## The challenge

A clinical lab needed to stop assembling reports by hand: capture each test's results, produce a professionally formatted document and deliver it to the patient with no manual steps.

## The solution

- **NestJS + MongoDB (Mongoose) API**, organized into patients, test templates, reports, contacts, users and mailer modules.
- **JWT authentication** (Passport), guards and a serialization interceptor so responses never leak sensitive fields.
- **Test templates** with parameters and reference ranges, so every result is captured against its normal range.
- **A4 PDF reports** rendered with **Puppeteer** and emailed with **Nodemailer**.
- **Angular + Angular Material frontend** with views for patients, tests, templates, reports, users and settings.
- **Docker** and docker-compose for both services, with Nginx serving the frontend.
