## The challenge

Manually capturing data from hundreds of policies from different insurers (policy number, term, premiums, insured party, agent…) is slow and error-prone. Sending every full PDF to an AI model solves it, but it's costly and unnecessary for known formats.

## The solution

A **hybrid, rules-first architecture**:

1. **Per-insurer rules**: detects which company issued the policy and extracts fields with templates for five insurers.
2. **Business validations**: checks that amounts add up (net premium + fees + surcharges − discounts + tax = total), dates and the insured's name, and flags missing or doubtful fields.
3. **Claude only for what's left**: requests just those fields via **tool use with a forced `tool_choice`** and a JSON schema generated from **Pydantic**. To save tokens it sends only the text of the relevant pages plus the values already extracted; for unknown insurers it sends the full text, and the PDF as a document only when it's scanned and has no text layer.

Also:

- **FastAPI** with a single-file endpoint and **batch processing** (up to 50 PDFs) streamed as **NDJSON**, with parallel AI calls under a concurrency limit.
- Every response reports the **method used** (rules, AI or both), warnings and tokens consumed.
- **Golden tests** comparing the AI against verified values, plus rule and batch tests.
- API key authentication with constant-time comparison, deployed on Render.
