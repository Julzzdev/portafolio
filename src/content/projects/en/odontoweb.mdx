## The challenge

Small dental clinics often keep their schedule on paper or spreadsheets and their clinical records in printed forms. They needed a single cloud tool each clinic could subscribe to based on its needs.

## The solution

- **Multi-clinic SaaS** on **Supabase (PostgreSQL)**: each clinic only sees its own data thanks to **Row Level Security**, and each plan unlocks modules by tier (the same function gates access in the database and in the UI).
- **Team roles** (admin and dentist), per-plan member limits and email invitations.
- **Step-by-step clinical records** validated with **Zod + React Hook Form**, with diagnosis and progress **dental charts** and version history.
- **Scheduling** color-coded per dentist, **appointment requests from the clinic's website** and a public page where patients confirm their appointment.
- **Automated email reminders** via an Edge Function scheduled with `pg_cron`.
- **Per-clinic branding**: name, logo and colors applied to the app theme.
- **React Router 7 in framework mode (SSR)**, feature-based architecture (domain, server and UI kept apart) and **Docker** deployment.
