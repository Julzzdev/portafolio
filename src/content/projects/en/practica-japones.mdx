## The challenge

Studying kanji with traditional flashcards is slow and not very interactive. I wanted my own tool to review readings and strokes at my own pace.

## The solution

- SPA built with **React 19 + TypeScript** and **Vite**.
- Practice by **levels**, with cards showing *on* / *kun* readings and meaning.
- Answer input with automatic romaji → kana conversion powered by **WanaKana**.
- **Drawing canvas** to practice each kanji's strokes.

## Outcome

A fast study tool I use daily, and a great playground to practice state management and interactive components in React.
